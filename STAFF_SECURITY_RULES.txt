BOOST QUOTE - STAFF SECURITY RULES
Suksun Inc.

For all authorized users:

1. Do not share the application outside the company.
2. Do not share internal device pricing, promotion rules, or inventory with unauthorized persons.
3. Protect your phone/computer with a screen lock.
4. Keep Safari/Chrome/Edge and the operating system updated.
5. Do not enter Social Security numbers, passwords, payment-card numbers, bank information,
   or government ID numbers into the quote notes.
6. Verify all promotions and eligibility in the official authorized system before final sale.
7. Do not promise a device merely because the quote app says "In Stock"; confirm inventory.
8. Do not install or run modified copies of the app.
9. Report lost devices, suspicious behavior, incorrect pricing, or suspected unauthorized access.
10. Delete local customer quote information when it is no longer needed under company policy.
