BOOST QUOTE APPLICATION
INTERNAL TERMS OF USE
Suksun Inc.
Effective Date: August 8, 2026

By using the Boost Quote Application, an authorized employee or contractor agrees to
use it only for legitimate Suksun Inc. business.

1. PURPOSE
The primary purpose of the application is to create accurate customer quotations for
wireless devices, plans, promotions, protection, accessories, fees, discounts, and
related retail transactions.

2. AUTHORIZED USERS
Only employees, contractors, managers, or other users specifically authorized by
Suksun Inc. may use the application.

3. QUOTE VERIFICATION
A displayed quote is an estimate until the transaction is confirmed in the applicable
authorized Boost Mobile system. Before completing a sale, staff must verify:
- Device availability.
- Customer and port-in eligibility.
- Required rate plan.
- Required prepaid service period.
- Add-a-line, upgrade, non-port, trade-in, and other promotion conditions.
- Taxes and applicable fees.
- Financing eligibility and terms, when relevant.
- Promotion dates and any store, market, or channel restrictions.

4. INVENTORY DISPLAY
"In Stock" status is a convenience feature for sales presentation. Staff must physically
or operationally confirm inventory before promising a device to a customer.

5. EOL DEVICES
Devices designated as end-of-life (EOL) are not intended to appear in the active
promotional catalog unless management expressly authorizes an exception.

6. CUSTOMER INFORMATION
Collect only the customer information needed for the quote or transaction. Do not use
customer information for personal purposes. Do not share it through unauthorized apps,
personal accounts, or unauthorized recipients.

7. DEVICE SECURITY
Staff should:
- Use a device protected by a passcode, PIN, password, biometric lock, or equivalent.
- Keep the operating system and browser updated.
- Avoid using the application on public or shared devices that are not company-approved.
- Lock the device when leaving it unattended.
- Report lost devices or suspected unauthorized access promptly.

8. LOCAL DATA
The current static version stores certain settings, inventory selections, and quote
information locally in the browser used by that employee. Clearing browser/site data,
changing browsers, or replacing the device may remove locally stored information.

9. NO SHARING OR COPYING
Employees may not distribute the application file, deployment package, source code, or
internal pricing information outside authorized Suksun Inc. personnel.

10. SECURITY
Users may not attempt to disable security protections, alter program logic for
unauthorized purposes, install malicious code, or access data beyond their authorization.

11. BUSINESS RECORDS
Where a quote becomes part of an official transaction or customer record, staff must
follow the company's approved record-retention and transaction procedures. The
application's local quote history should not be treated as the sole official business
record.

12. TERMINATION OF ACCESS
When a person's authorization ends, that person must stop using the application and
delete company copies or data from personally controlled devices when instructed.

13. CHANGES
Suksun Inc. may update these terms, the application, pricing data, promotion data,
inventory rules, and access requirements at any time.
