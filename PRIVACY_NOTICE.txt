BOOST QUOTE APPLICATION
INTERNAL PRIVACY NOTICE
Suksun Inc.
Effective Date: August 8, 2026

This notice describes the data handling of the current Boost Quote Application used
internally by authorized Suksun Inc. staff.

1. INFORMATION THAT MAY BE ENTERED
Depending on how an employee uses the quote form, the application may process:
- Customer name.
- Customer phone number or other contact information entered by staff.
- Device, rate plan, protection, accessory, and promotion selections.
- Trade-in, discount, fee, tax, and quote information.
- Sales representative information.
- Notes entered into a quotation.
- Local stock-selection settings.

2. PURPOSE
The information is used to prepare, review, save, print, copy, or share a customer
quotation and to support legitimate retail sales activity.

3. CURRENT STORAGE MODEL
The current v3.4 static application is local-first. Quote history, application settings,
and stock selections may be stored in the browser/device being used. The current
version does not provide a centralized Suksun Inc. cloud database or automatic
cross-device synchronization.

4. PHONE-NUMBER REDACTION
Where supported by the current application workflow, saved quote records may redact or
limit retained phone-number information. Employees should nevertheless minimize the
personal information entered and retained.

5. SHARING
Employees may share a quote only with the customer, authorized coworkers, or other
authorized recipients for legitimate business purposes. Customer data must not be sent
to personal accounts or unauthorized third parties.

6. LOCAL DEVICE RISK
Because data may reside in browser storage:
- Anyone with unauthorized access to an unlocked device may potentially access locally
  stored information.
- Clearing browser data may delete saved information.
- Browser/device backups may be governed by the device owner's platform settings.
Authorized users must secure their devices appropriately.

7. DATA MINIMIZATION
Do not enter Social Security numbers, passwords, full payment-card numbers, banking
credentials, government identity-document numbers, or other highly sensitive data into
the quote application.

8. RETENTION
Keep quote information only as long as required for legitimate business purposes and
company policy. Delete unnecessary local quote records when they are no longer needed.

9. SECURITY INCIDENTS
Suspected unauthorized access, loss of a device containing quote information, or
suspected disclosure of customer information should be reported promptly to management.

10. FUTURE HOSTED VERSION
If Suksun Inc. later deploys centralized accounts, cloud storage, database services,
analytics, or external integrations, this privacy notice should be updated before those
features are put into production.

11. CUSTOMER-FACING DISCLOSURES
This internal notice is not a substitute for any privacy notice, consent, disclosure,
or carrier requirement that applicable law, Boost Mobile, or another service provider
requires to be given to a customer.
